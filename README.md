# HR Analytics: Employee Compensation, Leave, Performance & Retention (2021–2025)

End-to-end HR data analysis project evaluating **35,290 employee snapshot records** (2021–2025), covering **538 active employees** and **217 resigned/terminated cases**[cite: 5, 6, 8, 9].

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning, grouping (`Performance Group`, `Leave freq group`, `Tenure group`), and statistical distribution checks (`NORM.DIST` on `engagement_score` vs. `risk_of_exit_score`)[cite: 2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 9].
- **Tableau Public:** Interactive HR dashboard and analytical visualizations[cite: 6, 7, 8, 10].
- **PowerPoint / PDF:** Final project slide deck and business recommendations[cite: 9].

## 📊 Key Findings
1. **Attrition Hotspots:** Highest resignation volume occurs in **Engineering** and **Sales**, with notable early turnover in the `< 1 Year` tenure group[cite: 1, 2, 5, 6].
2. **Leave Frequency vs. Performance & Risk:** Higher leave frequency (`High >= 5`) does not lower average performance (`~3.05`), while `Risk of Exit Score` (`41.86%`) shows only a narrow difference compared to lower-leave groups (`39.54%`–`39.75%`)[cite: 3, 5, 7].
3. **Retention Across Performance Groups:** Retention is highest in the **Low** performance group (**76.09%** active; **23.91%** terminated), whereas **Medium** (**68.18%** active; **31.82%** terminated) and **High** (**70.51%** active; **29.49%** terminated) show higher exit rates[cite: 8].

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- **Improvement of Onboarding Program:** Strengthen 6–12 month mentorship in departments with high early turnover (`< 1 Year`)[cite: 9].
- **Alignment of Reward & Merit Increase Schemes:** Differentiate compensation and promotion pathways for high performers[cite: 9].
- **Leave & Risk-Based Early Warning System:** Monitor employees with `>= 5` leave occurrences and elevated exit risk via regular 1-on-1 check-ins[cite: 7, 9].
- **Targeted Retention Review in Engineering & Sales:** Review workload and exit insights in Engineering and Sales to address role-specific attrition drivers[cite: 1, 9].
